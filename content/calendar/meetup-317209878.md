PowerShell Ohio User Group
PowerShell Universal is a web-based platform for PowerShell Developers that enables you to build REST API endpoints, Automation workflows, and Web Apps, all in the language you love: PowerShell.

In this session, we'll take a look at how you can extend PowerShell Universal with your own custom components. We'll learn what a component is, the requirements for building one, and demonstrate a few to spark your own creative ideas for your use cases! We'll also touch on the publishing process to make the components available to anyone (don't worry, it's super simple). See ya there!
