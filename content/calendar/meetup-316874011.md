Research Triangle PowerShell Users Group
It’s time for another **Open Mic Night** with the RTPSUG!

This isn’t your typical slide-deck meetup — it’s a relaxed evening of conversation, sharing, and community. Come hang out with fellow PowerShell users to talk about what you’ve been working on, cool things you’ve learned, or questions you’ve been thinking about.

There’s no agenda and no pressure to present — just open discussion and good company. Whether you want to show a quick script, get feedback on an idea, or simply listen and chat, everyone’s welcome.

Bring a script you're proud of, a problem you're wrestling with, or just yourself — we're here for all of it. This is your chance to learn what others are building, get real-time feedback, or discover that one cmdlet you didn't know existed.

**Ideas for the night:**

* Share what’s new in your PowerShell world
* Ask for tips or feedback on a challenge
* Talk about tools, modules, or automation wins
* Or just kick back and join the conversation

Come for the PowerShell — stay for the community.
