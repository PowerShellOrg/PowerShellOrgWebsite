I'm a systems engineer with 15 years in IT operations, more than 10 of them in
enterprise endpoint management on Windows and Linux. Most of my PowerShell goes
into patching, event log analysis, and fleet scripting.

I build [ET Ducky](https://etducky.com), an RMM that reads the kernel event
stream on each endpoint (ETW on Windows, eBPF on Linux). My open source tools,
including ProcDelta and ProviderExplorer, are on [GitHub](https://github.com/trucule).
