# PowerShell.org Website

The Hugo site and content pipeline for powershell.org, including community
article submissions, events, and author profiles.

## Language

**AI slop**:
Article content whose prose reads as predominantly AI-generated with little
to no human revision or firsthand verification — generic filler phrasing, no
concrete scenario or version the author actually hit, and code that reads as
untested.
_Avoid_: Vibe coded (describes the authoring process; "AI slop" describes the
resulting content)

**Vibe coded**:
The authoring process behind AI slop: content produced by prompting an AI
and submitting the output with little to no review, testing, or revision by
the human submitter.

**Off-topic pitch**:
An article submission that uses PowerShell as a backdrop to promote the
author's own site, product, or methodology, rather than keeping PowerShell
usage as the throughline. Distinct from AI slop — a pitch can be entirely
human-written and still be an off-topic pitch.
_Avoid_: Self-promotion (too broad — linking your own site in passing is
fine; the issue is thin PowerShell content relative to the promotion)
