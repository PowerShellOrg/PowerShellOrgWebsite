James Petty is the CEO of The DevOps Collective, Inc. and helps run PowerShell.org.
He is also the conference chair for [The PowerShell Summit](https://powershellsummit.org). A 7x Microsoft MVP, he is a co-author of
*PowerShell in a Month of Lunches* and *Learn PowerShell Scripting in a month of Lunches*

He is passionate about the PowerShell community — speaking at conferences and
user groups, writing, and helping others get the most out of PowerShell and
DevOps automation.
