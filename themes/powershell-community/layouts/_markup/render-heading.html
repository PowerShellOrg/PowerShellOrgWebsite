{{- /* Heading render hook: makes article headings directly linkable. Renders the heading with its auto-generated id (from .Anchor, i.e. Goldmark's autoHeadingID) plus a hover-revealed anchor link, so any section can be linked to and shared. Because the id still comes from .Anchor, pre-existing `#section` links keep resolving unchanged. The `{.class}` attribute authors add (e.g. .wp-block-heading) is preserved. Styling lives in static/css/heading-anchors.css. */ -}} {{- $id := .Anchor -}} {{- $class := "ps-heading" -}} {{- with .Attributes.class }}{{ $class = printf "%s %s" $class . }}{{ end -}} {{- .Text | safeHTML -}}
