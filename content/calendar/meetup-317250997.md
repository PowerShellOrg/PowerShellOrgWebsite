PowerShell UserGroup Inn-Salzach
Join us for an exciting PowerShell UserGroup InnSalzach session where we dive into the art of scripting!

**Topic:** AI for Admins No Framework, No Agent Zoo – Just Start Small
**Speaker:** Andreas Jordan
**Date:** 12th November 2026
These days, everyone is talking about AI frameworks, agents, and homegrown toolchains. This talk takes the opposite approach. I’ll show how I started handing real work off to AI with nothing more than a Claude subscription.

My core focus is data and databases, but for my lab I’m also part-time Windows administrator. I understand how the systems work in principle, but I often lack the details: the exact command or the right parameters. AI now fills that gap. Live in my lab, I’ll show examples from everyday admin work: making sense of cryptic error messages, working through other people’s scripts, and using modules whose commands I don’t know by heart. Or simply finding out where all the disk space went.
This is explicitly not about vibe coding. AI doesn’t replace my understanding. Mostly, it saves me typing, troubleshooting, and reading documentation. I can read, understand, and adapt the code it produces, and I do. To close, I’ll look at where the journey leads if you stick with it. These days, I’m happy to let Claude run my Ansible deployments.
