**No more, module not found (Stephan van Rooij)**
"Module not found" or "Function not loaded" were common errors when importing Microsoft.Graph.Authentication

This session will be about dependency management in PowerShell 7. Why are these errors there, how can we resolve then? What is the AssemblyLoadContext?

You will also get an answer to the question: why did all these issues disappear with version 2.41.0 and why it took Microsoft at least 18 months to not even fix it themself.

You guested it, this is a deep dive into dependency loading by a Microsoft MVP who single handedly fixed the dependency issues with Microsoft.Graph.Authentication by creating a pull request with the fix.

**Building Reliable PowerShell Automation with AI (Zoran Despotovski)**
Explore how a PowerShell script evolves into reliable production automation, using Azure tag synchronization as a practical case study. Learn approaches to testing, safe execution, and repeatable workflows, and how AI agents support development while human review and validation remain essential. Apply these lessons to your own PowerShell automation.

**About Zoran:**
Zoran Despotovski is Solution architect at at Team Rockstars IT, with more than 10 years of experience in development, infrastructure and cloud, including PowerShell and Azure automation.
He recently focuses on turning operational tasks into repeatable, testable workflows, with attention to real-world data, safe execution, and recovery from failures.
In this session, he shares lessons from developing production PowerShell automation with AI agents, human review, and practical validation.
