The Swiss PowerShell User Group brings together PowerShell enthusiasts, IT professionals, and automation fans from across Switzerland.

Join us for in-person meetups in Bern, Zurich, and other cities, as well as virtual sessions accessible from anywhere.

Whether you're just getting started with PowerShell or automating entire environments, you'll find a welcoming community ready to share knowledge, experiences, and ideas.

⚡ PowerShell & Automation
🤝 Networking with fellow IT professionals
🍕 Pizza, drinks, and great conversations
💻 In-person and virtual events

Follow us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/swiss-psug/posts/?feedView=all) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/swiss-psug.bsky.social), and join us for PowerShell fun, good company, and plenty of opportunities to learn and connect.
